One of the greatest perks of studying at CityU is how easy it is to escape Hong Kong for the weekend. The tourist version of Asia is nice once, but this is the student version: budget flights, hostels, map apps, and seamless cross-border travel that won't destroy your bank account.

  • Use skyscanner/momondo/kiwi.com for scanning flights so you can book the cheapest one. Pay attention to the fees that some airlines add later such as HK express with the fuel surcharge and different payables (the scanner websites usually include the fees so they're perfect for comparison across airlines).
  • Be mindful about the navigation as well, Google Maps doesn’t work in every country, use A-Map for Mainland trips and Naver-Map for South-Korea.
  • Another useful application is trip.com for booking accommodation or getting passes for activities, museums.
  • For payments use AliPay or WeChatPay as they are highly accepted in the region and your card might not work everywhere. Setting them up is self-explanatory and only takes a few minutes.
  • For mobile data the easiest option is an e-sim which you can install even before the trip. MobiMatter, Holafly or Saily are all great options, check out the packages they offer and pick the one that fits your destinations and schedule the best. Most of them offer Asia packages so you can cover all the countries you want to visit in one.
