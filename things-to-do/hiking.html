Hiking: The Free Activity Locals Love

Around 40% of Hong Kong is country park. You can be on a mountain trail 30 minutes after class.

Three hikes to start with

  • Lion Rock. The closest big hike to CityU, right above Kowloon Tong. It's steep, takes about 2 hours return, and the view over Kowloon is one of the best in the city.
  • Dragon's Back. An easy ridge walk on Hong Kong Island that ends near Big Wave Bay beach. Hike, then swim.
  • Sunset Peak (Lantau). Hard, but it's the classic sunset hike.

Stay safe

Bring at least 1.5 litres of water, start early in summer, and tell someone where you're going. Don't hike when the Hong Kong Observatory has a typhoon or very hot weather warning.
