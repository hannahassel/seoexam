Making Friends in Hong Kong: The Real Survival Guide

You can survive Hong Kong alone. But the friends you make here are what you'll remember in ten years.

How it works

  • Week one is everything. Everyone is new and looking for friends for about two weeks. Say yes to everything. The people you meet in week one are often your group by week four.
  • Join one society. Hiking, drama, board games, dance. It's the easiest way to meet local students, not just other exchange students.
  • Play a sport. A casual football, basketball or volleyball game is social life with zero small talk needed.
  • Eat with people. “Want to grab lunch at the canteen?” after a tutorial is how most friendships start.
  • Use the exchange group chats. Facebook and WhatsApp groups are where spontaneous beach days and Happy Valley trips get planned.
