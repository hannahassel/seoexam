Things to Do in Hong Kong as a Student (Without Going Broke)

The tourist version of Hong Kong is temples, theme parks and the harbour. Fine, once. If you're here for a semester, you need the version you'll repeat: free beaches, a HK$10 night at the races, the cheapest way up the Peak, hikes with skyline views, and nights out that don't empty your bank account.

Everything here is tested on a student budget. Prices were checked in November 2026.

  • Hong Kong's beaches → Shek O, Big Wave Bay, Repulse Bay and Clear Water Bay: free entry, cheap buses.
  • Horse racing at Happy Valley → The best HK$10 night out, every Wednesday from September to July.
  • Victoria Peak → Three ways up, what they cost, and the free view at the top.
  • Hiking → Lion Rock, Dragon's Back and Sunset Peak, and how to hike safely.
  • Going out → Lan Kwai Fong, Wan Chai and the TST waterfront without going broke.
  • Making friends → The real survival guide: week one, societies, sport and group chats.

FAQ

What are cheap things to do in Hong Kong for students?
Go to a free beach like Shek O, enter the Happy Valley races for HK$10 on a Wednesday, take Bus 15 up the Peak for HK$13, hike Lion Rock, or watch the free Symphony of Lights at 8pm.
How much does it cost to go to the horse races in Hong Kong?
Public entry at Happy Valley is HK$10, paid by Octopus card. You don't have to bet.
What is the cheapest way to get to Victoria Peak?
Citybus 15 from Central costs HK$13. Walking up Old Peak Road is free and takes about an hour.
Are Hong Kong beaches free?
Yes. Public beaches have free entry, and most have free changing rooms and showers.
