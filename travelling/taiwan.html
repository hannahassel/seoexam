Taiwan: The Optimal Destination

Whatever you are looking for you can find it there. Starting from the buzzing night markets with amazing street food to swimming with sea turtles on an island Taiwan has it all. Just a quick one and a half hour flight away lies one of the best destinations for students. The country is affordable, the activities are endless and the time you saved travelling makes it possible to have the most memorable long weekend of your life.
You can fly to Taipei or to Kaohsiung but you can even connect the two with a train ride on the iconic high speed trains that gets you from one city to another in less than two hours.
