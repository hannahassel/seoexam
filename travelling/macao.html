Macao: A Quick Historical Escape

The perfect location for an effortless day-trip. Just a short ferry ride or a bus across the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge away, Macao swaps Hong Kong's neon-lit vertical sprawl for pastel-colored colonial Portuguese plazas, historic ruins, and world-famous Portuguese egg tarts. It is compact enough to explore entirely on foot or using free casino shuttle buses, making it the ultimate low-cost cultural weekend break for exchange students. Bring cash if you want to spend because your usual apps and cards aren’t reliable there.
