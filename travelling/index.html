The Regional Travel Guide for Exchange Students

One of the best parts of studying at CityU is how easy it is to leave Hong Kong for a weekend. This is the student version of Asia: budget flights, cheap stays and trips that won't wreck your bank account.

  • Mainland China: Shenzhen and Guangzhou → Visas, apps and payments, getting across the border, and what to do in both cities.
  • Macao: a quick historical escape → Ferry or bridge bus, the old town on foot, egg tarts and free casino shuttles.
  • Taiwan: the optimal destination → Taipei and Kaohsiung, night markets, hot springs and swimming with sea turtles.
  • Southeast Asia: Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines → The Ha Giang loop, a crazy weekend in Bangkok and island-hopping in the Philippines.
  • South Korea: the weekend trip to Seoul → The apps to download, the airport transfer, T-money and what to do in Seoul.

Booking cheap flights

  • Compare first. Use Skyscanner, Momondo or Kiwi.com. Set the destination to “anywhere” and you can find flights from around HK$600–800.
  • Book early if you can. Prices rise close to departure, but spontaneous trips can still be cheap.
  • Watch the extras. Some airlines, like HK Express, add fuel surcharges and other fees later. Comparison sites usually include them.
  • Pick stays carefully. Airbnb, Booking.com and Trip.com all work. Trip.com is also good for passes and museum tickets.

Maps, money and mobile data

  • Navigation. Google Maps doesn't work everywhere. Use AMap on the mainland and Naver Map in South Korea.
  • Read real reviews. Google star ratings can mislead. Check the number of reviews and what people wrote, especially about bathrooms and cleanliness.
  • Payments. Alipay and WeChat Pay are widely accepted in the region, but still carry cash. Check ATM and foreign card fees with your bank. EUR and USD are the easiest to exchange.
  • Data. An e-SIM is easiest and can be installed before you leave. MobiMatter, Holafly and Saily offer Asia packages. Convenience stores sell physical SIMs.

Travel smart

  • Go in a group. Splitting stays, rides and food saves money, and it's safer. Join friends' plans through the exchange group chats.
  • Get travel insurance, and add cover for activities like scuba diving. Deals depend on your home country and destination.
  • Tell CityU where you're going. The university will email a form to fill in, so they know where you are if something happens.

FAQ

What are the cheapest weekend trips from Hong Kong for students?
Shenzhen and Macao are the easiest and cheapest, and budget flights to Taiwan, Bangkok or Seoul can start around HK$600–800.
How do I find cheap flights from Hong Kong?
Use Skyscanner, Momondo and Kiwi.com for comparing flights, search with a flexible destination, and check airline surcharges.
Which apps do I need for travelling in Asia?
AMap and DiDi for mainland China, Naver Map and K.Ride for Korea, Line for Taiwan, and Grab for Southeast Asia. Alipay and WeChat Pay help throughout the region.
What is the best e-SIM for Asia?
MobiMatter, Holafly and Saily all offer Asia packages. Compare them by destinations and trip length.
Do I need to tell my university before I travel?
Yes. CityU will email you a form to fill in with your travel plans.
