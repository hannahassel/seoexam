Add/Drop at CityU: Get the Timetable You Actually Want

Course registration at CityU runs through AIMS, and the add/drop period in the first two weeks of term is where timetables get fixed — or ruined. Here's the playbook:

  • Register the moment your slot opens. Popular electives fill within minutes. Have five backups ready, not two.
  • On a waitlist? Show up anyway. Attend the first lecture and ask the lecturer after class — they can often add you if there's space.
  • Check for timetable clashes yourself. AIMS won't always warn you nicely, and two wanted courses overlapping is your problem to solve. Some courses record lectures — check before you commit.
  • Deadlines are strict. After the add/drop cutoff you're locked in (and may still pay). Put the dates in your calendar on day one.

Exchange student with a required course that's full? Email your exchange coordinator early — they unstick this exact problem every semester, but only if you ask in time.
