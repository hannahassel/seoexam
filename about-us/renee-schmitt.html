Renée-Maria Schmitt

Hi, I’m Renée! I’m doing my Master’s in Information Systems at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), and this semester I’m on exchange at City University of Hong Kong. At TUM I’m also a scholarship holder at the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence for Reliable AI, and I’m planning to write my Master’s thesis on reliable and medical AI. Before that I did my Bachelor’s in Information Systems at the University of Münster, where I wrote my thesis on explainable AI and whether it can make AI more ethical.

Most of my work experience has been somewhere between tech and business. I’ve interned at KPMG in digital compliance, at Deutsche Telekom researching AI ethics, at BMW building a forecasting tool for spare parts, and at Accenture in analytics. During my Master’s, three classmates and I built WiseUp, a web platform that matches startups with retirees who want to share their expertise. We took it from first idea to a working app, and I learned a lot doing it.

When I’m not studying, I’m usually doing some kind of sport. I’ve played hockey and squash for years and I’ll say yes to pretty much anything active. My calmer side is all about making things by hand: I knit, sew some of my own clothes and draw whenever I find the time. I also play the flute. I spent a school year in Dublin and have travelled quite a bit since then, so I know how confusing the first weeks in a new country can be. That’s why I’m writing the University Life guides: finding your way around CityU, choosing courses and getting through add/drop shouldn’t take you as long as it took me.
