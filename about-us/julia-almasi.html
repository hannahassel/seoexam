Julia Almasi

My name is Julia and I’m a Data Science in Business student at Corvinus University of Budapest. Currently I’m doing my exchange semester at City University of Hong Kong at the College of Business. I wanted to explore Asia so I chose a location that was well connected and made travelling to other countries during the weekends easier. I fell completely in love with the city and the way of living. Outside of school and travels you will find me hiking around the city. The routes are simply amazing and the views you get are breathtaking.

I got to explore a lot of Asian countries during my stay, and I loved every single one of them. The different cultures, foods and activities you get to do don’t compare to what I was used to. The people I travelled with made the trips incredible, but the solo trips I took were all journeys as well. The whole semester was so memorable that I know I will carry it with me for the rest of my days.

When I get back home, I would like to start my internship and, filled with all these experiences, bring a creative view into my work. My thesis is focused on urban mobility, specifically nighttime mobility in Budapest, and I would like to dive deeper into this aspect. I believe that with all the absorbed knowledge I will be able to bring a different perspective into my field.
