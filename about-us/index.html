Our mission

We are five international exchange students at City University of Hong Kong (CityU), and we want to help incoming exchange students get more than a brief introduction to their life in Hong Kong.

Written by students, for students, our guide shares what we learned the hard way about student housing and public transport, university life at CityU, eating out on a student budget and travelling from Hong Kong, so your exchange semester starts with fewer surprises.
