Add/Drop at CityU: How to Get the Timetable You Want

Course registration at CityU runs through AIMS, the student system. The add/drop period at the start of each semester is when timetables get fixed or ruined. In Semester A 2026/27, online undergraduate add/drop ran in August and September, and you get a personal time ticket in AIMS that says when you can register. Check your own dates in AIMS. They change every semester.

The playbook

  • Register the minute your time ticket opens. Popular electives fill in minutes. Have five backup courses ready, not two.
  • Know which courses are “web-enabled”. Exchange students can add or drop web-enabled courses online. For courses that are not, or that have restrictions, you submit an electronic form in AIMS and the department approves it.
  • Don't drop a pre-registered course to swap it. If you drop it, you may not be able to add it back online.
  • Check for timetable clashes yourself. AIMS won't always warn you clearly.
  • On a waitlist? Go to the first class anyway. Ask the lecturer at the end. They can sometimes add you if there is space.
  • The deadline is strict. After add/drop closes, you're locked in, and you may still pay for courses you drop late.

Exchange student with a required course that's full?

Email your exchange coordinator early. They fix this exact problem every semester, but only if you ask before the deadline.
