University Life at CityU: A Student's Guide to Your First Semester

CityU's campus is small, busy and wired straight into Kowloon Tong MTR station. That makes it one of the easiest universities in Hong Kong to reach, and one of the most confusing for your first week. This guide covers what we wish someone had told us: how to get from the train to your classroom, how the building codes work, how to win add/drop in AIMS, and what lectures and tutorials actually expect from you.

It is written by exchange and local students at City University of Hong Kong (CityU or CityUHK). For getting around the rest of the city, see our transportation guide. For housing, see student housing.

FAQ

How do I get from Kowloon Tong MTR to CityU?
Take Exit C to Festival Walk, go up to level LG1, and take the escalator next to shop LG1-10 into the CityU subway. It takes about 5 minutes and stays indoors the whole way.
When is the add/drop period at CityU?
It runs in the first weeks of each semester. Your exact registration time is shown as a time ticket in AIMS, so check AIMS and the CityU academic calendar for your semester.
Can exchange students take any course at CityU?
Not always. Some courses have restrictions or give priority to degree students. Web-enabled courses can be added online. Others need an electronic form in AIMS and department approval.
Do I have to attend tutorials at CityU?
Yes, in most courses. Tutorials often carry participation marks, and some lecturers deduct marks for absences.
