Mainland China: Shenzhen and Guangzhou

Being only a short train ride away makes these cities popular amongst the students for day-trips or weekend getaways. The MTR takes you right to the border and after crossing you can already start your shopping journey at one of the many malls of Shenzhen. Take the high-speed intercity train slightly further north to Guangzhou for incredible Cantonese dim sum, historical architecture, and vibrant student-budget markets that give you a true taste of mainland scale. China is extremely cheap especially compared to Hong Kong so be prepared to do a lot of shopping and taxi rides. DiDi is your best friend for ride hailing in China.
