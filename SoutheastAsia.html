Southeast Asia: Philippines, Vietnam,
Thailand

  • Southeast Asia is very popular amongst tourists for a reason. If you have the time to go for more than a long weekend you should definitely visit Vietnam. The Hi Giang loop is practically mandatory for everyone now. Definitely go with an empty suitcase because no matter how small the budget everyone can do at least a little shopping there.
  • If you want a crazy weekend definitely go to Bangkok, the only thing you have to tell the Tuk-Tuk driver is to take you to Khao San Road and you are in for an insane night. Grab is the local ride hailing solution. Traffic can get really bad so you might still want to keep public transportation in mind.
  • The white beaches of the Philippines are simply gorgeous. However, this trip might require slightly more planning. The flight to Manila is cheap but you have to catch another to get to one of the many islands. Cebu Pacific can get you to practically any island but for short distances keep the ferry in mind.
