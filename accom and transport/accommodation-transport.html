For incoming exchange students, the two main accommodation options are the student compound in Ma On Shan or arranging private housing in the city. Both have advantages and disadvantages, so it is worth thinking about your preferences well before arrival.

Transportation in the city is well developed, and every part of the city is accessible with some form of transport. The only thing you need is Octopus; no, not the animal.
