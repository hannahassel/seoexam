Navigating CityU - Read This Before Week One

CityU's campus is more compact than most HK unis, but the building codes will confuse you at first! The main academic buildings are AC1, AC2 and AC3 which are connected internally, so you can cross most of campus without going outside.

  • The famous crystalline building across the road is the Run Run Shaw Creative Media Centre → media students' home, everyone else's landmark. You reach it by a footbridge.
  • Download the campus map PDF offline. Mobile signal dips in the older stairwells.
  • Do a dry run to each classroom before semester starts. Room codes like "AC1-XXXX" make sense once someone explains them — ask at the info counter or any senior.

Budget 10 extra minutes in week one. By week three, you'll cut through Festival Walk on autopilot.
