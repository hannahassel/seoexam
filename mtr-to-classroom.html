From MTR to the CityU Classroom

CityU sits above Kowloon Tong station, and the route is almost embarrassingly easy once you know it: take the exit signed for Festival Walk, walk through the mall, and it connects directly into campus. Mostly covered the whole way — you can go from train to classroom without an umbrella.

  • Kowloon Tong is an interchange (Kwun Tong line + East Rail line), so it's reachable from almost anywhere. Check which platform your line arrives on — the station is long.
  • Don't follow Google Maps blindly to street level. The mall route is faster and air-conditioned; the street route involves stairs and traffic lights.
  • Timing: from the platform, budget ~5 minutes to campus proper, plus 5 more to your building if it's AC3.
  • Going to the Creative Media Centre? It's on the other side of Tat Chee Avenue — use the footbridge, don't jaywalk at rush hour.
