Renting Private Housing in the Urban Core

For students who prefer freedom, independence, and a shorter commute, renting private housing in areas like Kowloon Tong, Mong Kok, or Prince Edward is a popular alternative. While it requires navigating local real estate agents, security deposits, and utility bills independently, it places you right in the heart of the city, saving hours of weekly travel time and offering a more immersive local living experience.

The most effective way to find cheap rooms is to check groups on social platforms or follow the guidelines the university provides.

Flats are incredibly expensive in Hong Kong, but you can catch great deals if you keep your eyes open.
