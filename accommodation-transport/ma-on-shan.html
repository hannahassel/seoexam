Ma On Shan Student Compound

The student residence at Ma On Shan is approximately a 45-minute commute to campus and back. Despite this lengthy journey, many students opt for this option, making it the social hub for the semester. Living here guarantees built-in community events, instant neighbor friendships, and a vibrant dorm culture, though you will need to master your morning alarm clock and transit schedule to make morning lectures on time. On the way back from a night out, you can split the taxi fare with others because the car is guaranteed to be full. The competition amongst the halls gets super fun; be prepared to want to participate in every one of them.
