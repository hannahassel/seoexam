The Essential: Octopus Card

An Octopus card is essential for anyone who is visiting Hong Kong. It is the absolute lifeline of daily life in Hong Kong, used for everything from subway fares and convenience store purchases to laundry machines.

You can either get a physical card or an online version.

For topping up the physical cards, machines are located at every MTR station. You can top them up at the customer service counters or convenience stores as well.

  • For students there is a student Octopus card which you can acquire through the link the university provides (they’ll send it via email).
  • Be mindful about your travel plans; if you want to go to the mainland, it is highly recommended to get a UnionPay Octopus card.
  • There are two types of subsidy schemes worth considering.
    • If you have a normal Octopus card, you can claim back up to HK$400. It opens on the 16th of every month and you can collect it retroactive to three months ago. The closest machine is at Kowloon Tong near exit H.
    • If you have a student Octopus card, you unlock special public transport fare concessions across MTR lines, making city-wide travel significantly cheaper.
